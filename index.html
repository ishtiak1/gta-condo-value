How the estimate is built

This is an estimate for planning, not an appraisal. Area benchmarks are approximate 2025–26 resale condo apartment averages compiled from public TRREB market reports and sold data, and they move every month. Check current figures in TRREB Market Watch and confirm a value with a REALTOR® CMA or a licensed appraiser before buying, selling or refinancing. Land transfer tax uses Ontario rates plus the Toronto municipal tax for Toronto areas, before first-time buyer rebates.
